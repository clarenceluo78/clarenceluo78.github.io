About

I am a PhD student in Machine Learning at the University of Cambridge, advised by Prof. Mateja Jamnik. I am affiliated with Darwin College and the Department of Computer Science and Technology. Prior to this, I completed my MRes in Machine Learning at Imperial College London, supervised by Prof. Lucia Specia. I obtained my B.Eng. degree in Computer Science from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.

I have worked as an AI Associate at J.P. Morgan, designing and implementing LLM-based solutions for real-world financial applications. I previously interned at Microsoft Research Asia, Shanghai AI Lab, Tencent and Seasun Entertainment.

📢 2025 update: open to advise student projects on interp research. For Cambridge students, please see this link.

I study how machine learning models work, how to make them more reliable, and how to use them to solve interesting real-world problems. My recent work spans:

  • Model mechanisms, interpretability, and control: studying and manipulating the representations that shape language-model behavior [xLLM] [MoD] [SKOP] [Persistent SAE].
  • Applied ML and agentic systems: building scalable methods for document intelligence, automated systems, and domain-facing LLM applications [OpenFE] [ActiveAD] [TIDE] [PageIndex].
